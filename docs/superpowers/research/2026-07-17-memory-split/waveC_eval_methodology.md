FineWeb's explicit selection criteria are exactly yours: low variance across data/seed reruns, monotonic improvement during training, above-random scores at this scale; FineWeb2 ([2506.20920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20920)) adds "ordering consistency" as a fourth criterion.

At your 10–100B-token budget, expect the Pythia-1B column, plus a few points: HellaSwag ~45–55, ARC-E ~48–58, PIQA ~69–74, WinoGrande ~52–57, ARC-C ~26–32, OBQA ~30–37, CSQA ~32–40.

**What is dead at this scale:**
- **MMLU in multiple-choice format (MCF) is unusable.** Even 7B models trained to 210B tokens score 25.9 (chance = 25) with monotonicity ≈ 0.1, while the *cloze* formulation of the same benchmark scores 37.5 with monotonicity 0.79 (Madaan et al., [2406.10229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229), Tables 1–2). OLMES exists partly because of this: small base models must be evaluated with the completion/cloze formulation; OLMES runs both and takes the max ([2406.08446](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08446)). SmolLM2's MMLU MCF was still ~29.6 (near chance) after 6T tokens and only reached 48.9 at 11T. The loss-perspective emergence study ([2403.15796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15796), NeurIPS 2024) shows MMLU/C-Eval/GSM8K sit exactly at chance until pretraining loss ≈ 2.2, then climb — a 1B model at 10–100B tokens is above that loss. **Use MMLU-cloze (or CORRECT-PROB on MMLU) as a secondary metric only.**
- **GSM8K is floor.** 4.1 ± 0.5 at 7B/210B tokens ([2406.10229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229)); SmolLM2-1.7B was still at 4.3 after 6T generic tokens (it reaches 31 only after dedicated FineMath annealing). MATH ≈ 1.5, BBH/AGIEval ≈ chance, HumanEval < 12 at these scales. Natural math/code benchmarks cannot carry your reasoning claim.
- Benchmarks that improve *smoothly from the start* per [2403.15796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15796): TriviaQA, HellaSwag, RACE, WinoGrande — no emergence cliff.

**Decision-reliability evidence** — DataDecide (Ai2, [2504.11393](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11393), COLM 2025) is the single most relevant study: 25 corpora × 14 sizes (4M–1B) × **3 seeds**, all trained at 100 tokens/param (1B → 100B tokens, your exact regime), evaluated on OLMES. Findings: MMLU and ARC are the *cheapest to predict* (>80% pairwise decision accuracy using tiny models) **when using continuous likelihood metrics (CORRECT PROB)** rather than accuracy; HellaSwag needs more compute but is highly monotone; SocialIQA is hard to predict at all scales. Decision accuracy = spread across recipes vs run-to-run seed noise — the exact statistic your experiment needs. All 1,050 models + per-instance eval results are public (`allenai/DataDecide`).

## 2. Factual-recall measurement

- **Closed-book QA anchors at ~1B**: GPT-3 XL 1.3B/300B tokens ([2005.14165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165), App. H): TriviaQA EM 19.7 zero-shot / 32.1 few-shot; NQ-open 5.4 one-shot / 9.7 few-shot. Pythia-1B closed-book is a few EM points on both. At 10–100B training tokens expect TriviaQA ~5–15 EM, NQ-open ~1–5 EM — low but measurably above zero, and TriviaQA is among the *least* noisy generative tasks (seed σ = 0.45, monotonicity 0.99 at 7B; [2406.10229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229)). Score with lm-eval-harness EM/F1, 5-shot.
- **PopQA** ([2212.10511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10511), ACL 2023; data `akariasai/PopQA`): 14k entity questions with Wikipedia-pageview popularity labels. Purpose-built for your deliverable: accuracy-vs-popularity curves show parametric memory handles head entities while retrieval dominates the tail; retrieval-augmented GPT-Neo-1.3B matched GPT-3 on it. Report retrieval-ON vs OFF by popularity bin. EntityQuestions ([2109.08535](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08535)) is the companion long-tail set.
- **LAMA/T-REx cloze probing** ([1909.01066](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01066)): 41 Wikidata relations, "Dante was born in [MASK]", P@1. Cheap and knowledge-pure, but prompt-brittle (LPAQA [1911.12543](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.12543); "Factual Probing is [MASK]" [2104.05240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05240)) — use as a secondary probe, with identical prompts across arms.
- **Retrieval ON/OFF protocol (standard practice)**: RETRO ([2112.04426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04426)) is the template — the same trained checkpoint is evaluated as `Retro[ON]` and `Retro[OFF]` (retrieval pathway disabled); RETRO[OFF] ≈ parameter-matched dense baseline on bits-per-byte, and the paper's §2.6 introduces **leakage-aware evaluation**: plot eval loss as a function of eval-chunk overlap with the retrieval database, since retrieval models exploit train–test overlap much more strongly. Adopt this: your "matches dense on facts" claim needs the overlap-stratified curve, not just the aggregate. Same on/off protocol in kNN-LM ([1911.00172](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00172)), REALM ([2002.08909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08909)), Atlas ([2208.03299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.03299)), In-Context RALM ([2302.00083](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00083)), SILO ([2308.04430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04430)).
- **Synthetic recall (recommended primary instrument)**: the Physics-of-LM bioS biography testbed ([2309.14316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14316)) mixed into pretraining gives an exact, popularity-controlled measure of facts stored in parameters vs the store — see §5.

## 3. Reasoning evals learnable at ≤1B

Evidence that sub-1B models genuinely learn these (all trained-in, i.e., task data in the training mix — the right design at this scale, since zero-shot emergence of natural reasoning benchmarks is what you *can't* get):

- **iGSM synthetic grade-school math** (Physics of LM 2.1, [2407.20311](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20311), ICLR 2025): GPT-2-small (100M, RoPE) pretrained on iGSM reaches **99%** on unseen problems and length-generalizes beyond training reasoning depth; >90 trillion solution templates rule out memorization; **depth beats width** (16L×576d > 4L×1920d). Official generator: [facebookresearch/iGSM](https://github.com/facebookresearch/iGSM) (also Part 2.2, [2408.16293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16293)). Arithmetic is mod-23 to eliminate knowledge load. This is the strongest reasoning instrument for your experiment: parameterize difficulty by `op` (operation count) and report accuracy-vs-op curves.
- **RuleTaker deduction** ([2002.05867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05867)): RoBERTa-large (355M) hits 99% within-depth and 95%+ on deeper chains; even BERT gets 95%. **ProofWriter** ([2012.13048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13048)): T5-large (770M) nearly matches T5-11B on proof generation (D3→D5). Deduction over stated rules is comfortably sub-1B learnable and knowledge-free.
- **Multi-digit arithmetic** ([2307.03381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03381)): 10.6M–124M NanoGPT learns add/sub/mul/sqrt from scratch; reversed-digit output and scratchpad formats are essential; sharp 0→100% phase transitions. Caveat: length generalization is achievable but brittle ([2402.09371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09371)) — fix digit ranges and report in-distribution + OOD separately.
- **LEGO** ([2206.04301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04301)): chained variable-resolution; BERT-scale models learn it; good for probing shortcut vs compositional solutions. Sorting/copying/algorithmic tasks likewise (cf. Chomsky-hierarchy study [2207.02098](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02098) for which ops generalize).
- **bAbI** ([1502.05698](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05698)): essentially solved (~100%) with task-specific training at tiny scale; too easy as a headline metric but fine as a trained-in sanity probe.
- **Physics of LM 3.2 knowledge manipulation** ([2309.14402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14402)): classification/comparison over stored facts fail without CoT even when retrieval of those facts is perfect; inverse search is ~0% (the reversal curse, cf. [2309.12288](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12288)). Use manipulation-with-CoT accuracy as a "reasoning over knowledge" metric — and note the interesting prediction that your retrieval-arm may do *better* here because facts arrive in-context.
- **Natural, low-knowledge-load tasks** (zero-shot, above-noise at 1B): HellaSwag (best SNR of any benchmark), PIQA, Winogrande, LAMBADA, ARC-E; reading comprehension with provided context (SQuAD, CoQA — SmolLM2 tracks both; DROP for discrete numeric ops, but see clustering caveat below) is the natural analog of "reasoning with store access."

## 4. Statistical practice: seed noise, run counts, variance reduction

**Seed-variance numbers (the best available):**
- Madaan et al., "Quantifying Variance in Evaluation Benchmarks" ([2406.10229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229)): 10 identical Llama-2-7B pretrained from scratch (210B tokens) differing only in init seed. Final-checkpoint seed std (accuracy points): **HellaSwag 0.12–0.21, MMLU 0.49–0.57, ARC-C 0.80–0.87, PIQA ~0.4, SIQA ~0.5, TriviaQA 0.45, NQ 0.60, GSM8K 0.41–0.52, AGIEval ~0.8, HumanEval 1.1–1.75, COPA 2.0–2.2.** Per-model bootstrapped 95% CIs are larger (COPA ±8.3, ARC-C ±2.7, SIQA ±2.2, PIQA ±2.0, MMLU ±0.7, HellaSwag ±0.9). Small benchmarks (COPA n=100, HumanEval n=164) are noise-dominated — avoid. Continuous metrics raise SNR ~3–10× (HellaSwag SNR 608→1921) and monotonicity is higher for continuous metrics on *every* benchmark. Treat these 7B numbers as *lower bounds* at 1B.
- PolyPythias ([2503.09543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09543), ICLR 2025): 10 seeds × {14M–410M} Pythia (both init and data-order varied). Training dynamics are highly consistent across seeds, **but 2/50 runs (410M, seeds 3–4) are genuine outliers** with performance drops tied to "representation degeneration" — so use ≥3 seeds partly as outlier detection, and check per-seed curves before averaging. Evals used: ARC-E, LAMBADA, PIQA, SciQ (the subset above random at those sizes).
- DataDecide ([2504.11393](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11393)): **3 seeds** (init + data order) at every scale up to 1B/100B — the de-facto standard run count; FineWeb used 2 seeds and averaged; OLMo-2 ([2501.00656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00656)) runs its 50–100B mid-training 3× with different data-order seeds and **soups** the result. Comparable papers: 2–3 seeds, rarely more; 10 seeds is a dedicated variance study.

**Variance-reduction design (do all of these):**
1. **Same data, same order, paired arms**: fix tokenizer, sequence length, batch schedule, and data-order seed across dense vs retrieval arms so the only difference is the intervention (Paloma's comparability guidelines formalize this: fixed tokenization + data order + decontamination; [2312.10523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10523)).
2. **Paired statistics**: per Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals" ([2411.00640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640)) — question-level paired differences (score correlations 0.3–0.7 make this a free ~30–50% SE reduction), **clustered SEs** for passage-grouped evals (DROP/SQuAD/RACE naive SEs can be 3× too small), resampled answers, next-token-probability metrics, power analysis before you train.
3. **Continuous metrics**: CORRECT PROB / bits-per-byte as primary at small scale (DataDecide §3.3; Madaan; also the discrete-metric-mirage argument, Schaeffer [2304.15004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.15004)).
4. **Checkpoint averaging**: DataDecide averages the last 10% of checkpoints to kill step-to-step noise.
5. **Power rule of thumb**: with 3 seeds/arm and the σ's above, the minimal detectable effect (~2·σ·√(2/3)) is ≈ 0.3–0.5 pts on HellaSwag, ~1.5 pts on ARC-C, ~1 pt on TriviaQA EM, ~2–4 pts on small/generative tasks. Deltas smaller than that need more seeds or continuous metrics.

## 5. Knowledge-vs-reasoning decomposition and attribution

- **Physics of LM 3.3 capacity accounting** ([2404.05405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05405), ICLR 2025): transformers store a universal **2 bits/param** when facts are seen ~1000×, dropping to ~1 bit/param at 100 exposures, further degraded by junk-data dilution. This gives you the cleanest attribution instrument: inject a bioS corpus of *known bit-content*, then measure knowledge bits stored in each arm's parameters. "Freed capacity" becomes a measured quantity (dense stores X bits; retrieval-trained stores <X with no QA loss when the store is attached), not an inference from benchmark deltas. Official code: [facebookresearch/PhysicsLM4](https://github.com/facebookresearch/PhysicsLM4) (covers Parts 1, 3.1, 3.3, 4.x); independent replication of 3.1: [ykrmm/PhysicsLLM_3.1](https://github.com/ykrmm/PhysicsLLM_3.1) (GPT-2-124M, ~97% extraction with multi5+permute+fullname augmentation — augmentation is mandatory or extraction collapses to ~0%).
- **SynthWorlds** ([2510.24427](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24427), 2025): parallel real-mapped vs synthetic-mapped corpora with identical relational structure; defines the **"knowledge advantage gap"** between parametric-only and knowledge-augmented settings on mirrored multi-hop QA — directly reusable for your paired-arm design.
- Step-level decomposition metrics: **Information Gain vs Knowledge Index** ([2506.02126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02126)); benchmark splitting into knowledge- vs reasoning-subsets ([2505.11462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11462)); `<memory>`/`<reason>` decomposed inference ([2411.13504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13504), ACL 2025). Confound warnings: "reasoning" scores track pretraining term frequency (Razeghi, [2202.07206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07206)) and memorized task variants (counterfactual eval, "Reasoning or Reciting," [2307.02477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02477)) — another reason synthetic reasoning tasks carry the headline claim.
- **Architectural precedents for "externalized memory frees compute-capacity"**: Memory Layers at Scale ([2412.09764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09764)) — a 1.3B model + 128B memory parameters approaches Llama-2-7B on NQ/TQA, with gains *concentrated on factual tasks*; its eval split (factual: NQ, TQA, HotpotQA vs general: MMLU, HellaSwag, OBQA, PIQA vs code) is a ready-made template. Scaling retrieval datastores ([2407.12854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12854), NeurIPS 2024): Pythia-1B + 100B-token datastore **matches Pythia-12B** on TriviaQA/NQ, but retrieval barely helps MMLU-type tasks when the base model is weak — i.e., facts externalize easily; reasoning must live in the weights. RETRO ([2112.04426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04426)) for the on/off + leakage protocol.

## Recommended eval battery

**A. Fact-recall set** (each run with retrieval ON and OFF, identical prompts, paired per-question):
1. TriviaQA (full 11k dev, EM, 5-shot) — primary natural probe; expect ~5–15 EM closed-book at 1B/10–100B tokens (anchor: GPT-3 1.3B/300B = 32 few-shot), σ_seed ≈ 0.5.
2. NQ-open (EM, 5-shot) — harder tail; expect 1–5 closed-book.
3. PopQA + EntityQuestions, popularity-stratified — the headline "matches dense on facts with store access" figure.
4. bioS synthetic biographies mixed into both arms' pretraining ([2309.14316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14316)/[2404.05405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05405)) — extraction accuracy + bits/param capacity: the attribution measurement.
5. (Optional) LAMA-T-REx P@1 as a cheap prompt-fixed probe.

**B. Reasoning set:**
1. Trained-in synthetic (headline): iGSM accuracy-vs-op curves; RuleTaker/ProofWriter-style deduction by depth; 3–5-digit reversed arithmetic; LEGO chains. 10k+ test items each → CIs of a few tenths of a point, zero knowledge load.
2. Natural zero-shot (supporting): HellaSwag (acc_norm; highest SNR), ARC-E/C (cloze), PIQA, Winogrande, CSQA, OBQA, LAMBADA — the FineWeb/OLMES high-signal set; MMLU-cloze secondary; report OLMES-style CF formulation with CORRECT PROB alongside accuracy.
3. Reasoning-over-context: SQuAD/CoQA F1 (+ DROP with clustered SEs) — measures use of provided text, the skill your retrieval arm depends on.
4. Do **not** headline: MMLU-MCF, GSM8K, MATH, BBH, AGIEval, HumanEval, COPA (floor and/or noise at this scale).

**C. Held-out perplexity slices** (bits-per-byte, decontaminated, fixed data order — Paloma guidelines, [2312.10523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10523)):
- Paloma's 585-domain suite (or its 11 public sources);
- custom stratified slices: entity-dense factual text (Wikipedia bios, entity-rich news) vs low-fact text (fiction, dialogue) vs procedural (code/math) — prediction: retrieval-OFF hurts the factual slice specifically, not the others;
- RETRO-style leakage-stratified bpb (eval loss vs n-gram overlap with the retrieval store) to prove gains aren't copy-paste.

**D. Statistics:** 3 seeds per arm minimum (5 if affordable), same data order across arms, per-seed paired deltas, clustered+paired SEs per [2411.00640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640), checkpoint-window averaging, continuous metrics primary. Budget effects against the noise floor: seed σ ≈ 0.1–0.2 (HellaSwag), 0.4–0.9 (most MCQA/TQA), 1–2+ (generative/small sets) — i.e., a credible "beats dense on reasoning" needs ≥1–2 points on natural MCQA, but synthetic tasks with 10k items can certify sub-point deltas.

One caveat to flag: the per-benchmark seed-σ table comes from 7B/210B models (Madaan) because no published study reports per-benchmark seed variance at exactly 1B — DataDecide's 3-seed 1B/100B suite is public (`allenai/DataDecide-eval-results`), so you can compute those σ's directly before locking your power analysis.
